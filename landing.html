Why choose Journey into the Soil?

Because resilient farming begins deep within the root zone. By bridging NASA Earth observations—including soil moisture, rainfall anomalies, and solar irradiance—with local soil physics and agronomic science, our platform guides farmers toward balanced, water-smart crop rotations that restore soil vitality without guesswork or unrealistic promises.

Underground Realities

The Hidden Battlefield in the Soil and How to Defeat It

Beneath every thriving crop lies a continuous struggle: invisible salinity creeping up through irrigation water, silent nutrient depletion stripping fertility across back-to-back harvests, and hidden moisture stress restricting root expansion long before drought signs appear on the leaves. When rotation decisions are made in the dark, severe soil compaction and secondary salinization threaten entire agricultural livelihoods.

Journey into the Soil transforms this invisible underground dynamic into clear, science-backed guidance. By simulating multi-layer soil hydrology, root salt tolerance thresholds, and daily water balances alongside NASA satellite data, we empower farmers to defeat soil degradation, optimize every drop of irrigation, and secure land fertility for generations to come.

HOW IT WORKS

How does it work?

From field sample inputs to resilient rotation plans through physics-based simulation.

1

Collect Field Data

Input field location, soil texture by feel, water salinity, irrigation method, and recent crop rotation history.

2

Integrate NASA Observations

Aggregate daily solar radiation, ambient temperature, precipitation, and soil moisture from NASA POWER and Earth science models.

3

Simulate Soil Physics

Compute FAO-56 reference evapotranspiration, multi-layer water infiltration, drainage, and root salt accumulation dynamics.

4

Deliver Recommendations

Generate ranked crop rotation strategies matching farmer priorities for water saving, soil health, and risk mitigation.
