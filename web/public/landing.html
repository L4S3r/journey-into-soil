The Hidden Battlefield in the Soil

Deep beneath the green crops, an invisible war threatens every harvest. Here are the three critical underground battles raging in the soil.

BATTLE 01 · SALINITY SIEGE

Salt Strangulation

  • Salt builds up around the roots and stops them from drinking water.
  • The plant dries out and dies of thirst, even when the soil is wet.
COMBAT IMPACT Threat: Plants Thirsty in Wet Soil
BATTLE 02 · NUTRIENT AMBUSH

Nutrient Exhaustion

  • Planting the same crop year after year eats up all food in the dirt.
  • The soil gets tired and empty, leaving new crops nothing to grow on.
COMBAT IMPACT Threat: Soil Running Out of Food
BATTLE 03 · MOISTURE LOCKOUT

Hardpan Drought

  • Strong sun and dry heat bake the ground into a crust like concrete.
  • Water cannot soak deep down, and young roots hit a solid wall.
COMBAT IMPACT Threat: Roots Trapped by Hard Ground

Why was chickpea recommended after wheat in Beheira with 2.8 dS/m canal water?
👨‍🌾
🌱

Agronomic rationale: In Beheira's clay-loam soil, back-to-back cereal crops heavily exhaust available root-zone nitrogen. Chickpea fixes atmospheric nitrogen (approx. 40–60 kg N/ha), revitalizing soil biology.

Salinity buffer: Chickpea handles moderate root ECe thresholds up to 1.7 dS/m. With 2.8 dS/m irrigation water, our FAO-56 simulation mandates a 12% leaching fraction during winter rains to flush accumulated salts below the 45 cm root perimeter.

Advisory note: Always verify local drainage capacity before planting.
